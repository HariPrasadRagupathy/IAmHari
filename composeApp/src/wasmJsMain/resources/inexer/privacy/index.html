This Privacy Policy describes how Inexer (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) handles information when you use the Inexer mobile application. By downloading, installing, accessing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

Inexer is an income and expense tracking app. Your financial records are stored on your device in a local database. We do not operate a user account system that uploads your ledger to our servers.

Inexer is a personal productivity tool. It is not accounting, tax, investment, legal, or professional financial advice. You are solely responsible for how you use the App and for any decisions you make based on information shown in it.

Important — backup before updating Because your ledger lives on your device and is not synced to Inexer cloud servers, you must export or back up your data before installing a new release, reinstalling the App, clearing app storage, or changing devices. Updates and device actions can affect on-device data. If you do not keep a backup, you may permanently lose records, and we cannot restore them for you.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to the production Android app com.hp.inexer (and equivalent Inexer builds you install from official stores).

2. Information we process

2.1 Information you enter (stored on your device)

When you use Inexer, you may create:

  • Income and expense transactions (amounts, dates, categories, notes, spaces/budgets, and similar ledger data)
  • Optional images attached to transaction notes (for example photos taken with the camera or chosen from your device)
  • App settings, including an optional app-lock PIN
  • Export/import preferences (format, schedule, destination)

Where it is stored: on your device in Inexer’s local database and related app storage.

What we do not do with this ledger data: we do not sell it, and we do not upload your income/expense database to Inexer-operated servers for cloud sync as part of the current product.

You are solely responsible for protecting access to your device, your PIN/app lock if enabled, and any backups or exports you create.

2.2 Optional exports you choose

If you use export features, Inexer may write files (such as CSV) to storage on your device, or to another destination that you choose and authorize when such options are available. Those exports contain the financial data you choose to export and remain under your control.

We do not control third-party storage services you may use. Their terms and privacy practices apply to data you place there.

2.3 Diagnostics and app improvement (Firebase)

In production builds, Inexer uses Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and stability reports (to fix bugs)
  • Firebase Analytics — aggregated usage analytics (to understand feature usage)

These services may process technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, language, coarse diagnostics, and crash stack traces. They are provided by Google and subject to Google’s terms and privacy documentation for Firebase.

Important: Crashlytics/Analytics are for app quality and usage metrics. They are not used to host or sync your full income/expense ledger on our servers.

You can learn more in Google’s documentation for Firebase.

2.4 Permissions the App may use

Depending on features you use, Android may show permission prompts for:

Permission Why
Internet Contact Firebase (Crashlytics/Analytics) and any online features you enable
Camera Optional photos for transaction notes

We request permissions only as needed for those features.

2.5 Device backups

The Android app may allow system backup (allowBackup). If device backup/restore is enabled on your phone, app data could be included in your device backup according to your OS and account settings. System backup behavior is controlled by your device and Google account settings, not by Inexer support, and is not a substitute for exporting your own backup before an update.

2.6 App updates and data safety

Because Inexer stores your ledger locally:

  • Before updating, reinstalling, clearing data, or switching devices — export or otherwise back up your data from within the App.
  • After updating — confirm that your records look correct before entering new data.
  • If something looks wrong — keep your backup file safe. We may try to help with general guidance, but we cannot guarantee recovery of lost on-device data.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the App.

5. Data retention

  • Exports: retained wherever you saved them, under your control.
  • Firebase diagnostics/analytics: retained according to Google Firebase retention settings and practices.
  • Support emails: retained as needed to handle your request.

6. Security

We design Inexer so sensitive financial records stay in local device storage. Optional app lock (PIN) can add a barrier when opening the App on your device.

No method of storage or software is 100% secure or error-free. Protect your device with a screen lock, keep your own backups, and uninstall or clear data if you no longer want Inexer data on a device.
